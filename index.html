01Research

My research sits where memory meets computation. I work on SRAM and memristor compute-in-memory circuits, the timing and device variation that breaks them, and the neural networks that run on them.

Featured · Submitted to IEEE TCAS-I

TANDEM: a PVT-robust time-to-first-spike SRAM compute-in-memory core

What: A 7 nm ASAP7 8T SRAM compute-in-memory core that runs spiking neural networks, with on-chip calibration of timing and read charge.

Result: Correct decisions across all 27 process, voltage and temperature corners (17 of 27 without calibration). 9.45 pJ per frame at 0.57 V, 26% less energy than at 0.63 V.

Verification: Closed-loop SPICE–RTL co-simulation of 1,306 transistors, plus all 21 converged Monte Carlo samples.

Accuracy: 98.68% on MNIST and 93.12% on CIFAR-10 with circuit-fitted SNN evaluation.

03Projects

Each project lists its goal, the key result, and my role, so you can get the point without opening anything else.

SRAM CIMBNN45 nm

Variation-aware SRAM CIM for Binary Neural Networks

Goal: Run Boolean logic (AND, OR, XOR, XNOR) inside 6T SRAM arrays to cut data movement.

Result: Cutting XNOR error from 9.5% to 0.25% raised MNIST accuracy from 58.27% to 89.34%. About 3.17 pJ per CIM operation.

My role: Circuit design in Cadence Virtuoso, 1,200-point Monte Carlo analysis, and the PyTorch framework that links timing variation to accuracy.

SRAM-CAMFinFET7 nm

PRECIS-CAM: energy-efficient similarity search

Goal: Exact Hamming-distance search in SRAM-CAM with less sensing energy.

Result: Initial FF-corner simulations show about 38.6% lower sensing energy than forced fine sensing (controller excluded).

My role: Circuit side: 64-bit bank with 4-bit Hamming slices, parity-assisted sensing, and ngspice evaluation.

In progress · since Sep 2026

MemristorCIMLLM inference

Ternary memristive compute-in-memory for LLM inference

Goal: Ternary weights and ADC-free signed column readout on memristor crossbars.

Progress: Verified Verilog-A memristor models and three-level 1T1R programming in GPDK045/Spectre. Targeting the IHP SG13G2 memristor model.

Next: 64 × 64 crossbar and BitNet b1.58 evaluation of variation, energy and inference accuracy.

In progress · since Nov 2026

NeuromorphicRRAMPower gating

Normally-off LIF neuron with HfO₂ RRAM checkpointing

Goal: Keep a LIF neuron's membrane state in multi-level HfO₂ 1T1R cells so it can resume after power gating.

Plan: Compare 2-, 4-, 8- and 16-level storage for reconstruction error, spike dynamics and SNN accuracy. Derive the break-even idle time for checkpointing.

In progress · since Oct 2026
